# Moonshiner

A moonshine lab simulation for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**.

Drinks are not crafted here: you carry them through the whole process, and every stage affects the next one. Strength, quality and impurities travel from item to item and live in the bottle itself - miss the fermentation temperature and the wash comes out dirty; flip the receiver too early and the hearts are gone.

## Features

- **Nine production stages** - mash tun, fermentation barrel, pot still with optional heads/hearts/tails cuts or a whole run, rectification column, charcoal filter, dilution basin, five-slot blending table, aging barrel and an open moonshine crate for four bottles.
- **Technology defines the drink, not the player** - wort sets the possible range, fermented wash is a drinkable but rough spirit, fraction separation is optional and shapes the type, and the column matures the spirit over up to six passes.
- **Nine raw materials** - malt, wheat, sugar, sweet berries, honey, potato, beetroot, sugar cane and apples, each with its own quality bonus, origin and wash strength.
- **Five-star quality** - the internal 0-100 score is shown everywhere as 0-5 stars with halves, and any operation may be repeated: filter, blend, infuse, distill or age the same bottle as often as you like.
- **Process physics instead of a progress bar** - boiling point of the ethanol–water mix, vapour enrichment, approach to the azeotrope, fermentation temperature, mandatory water cooling, mass balance.
- **Data carried in the item** - strength, quality, impurities, origin, number of distillations, fraction-separation flag, column passes, filtration, age and blend composition survive every transfer and are stored in the bottle.
- **Procedural spirits** - 26 additives, up to five per blend, 192 name/aroma variants; the same set always yields the same drink, and blending keeps the base type.
- **A tasting table** - set a bottle in the drink slot and a hydrometer beside it to read the derived type, strength, quality and impurities, plus the intoxication level I-V, duration, infusion aura and any technical-fraction debuffs. The sample is never consumed.
- **Intoxication I-V** with nausea, vomiting (IV-V), staggering, drunken resilience, drunken strike, arrow deflection and four infusion types.
- **Nine working machines and a decorative condenser**, 33 items with original voxel models, parchment-and-oak GUI panels, an illustrated ten-chapter in-game guide with a recipe book that unlocks as you distil.
- **Russian and English** localization.

The mod is required on both client and server. Drop the JAR into the `mods` folder.

## Building

JDK 21 must be installed and `JAVA_HOME` set to it. From the project root:

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat build
```

The artifact appears in `build/libs/moonshiner-0.0.2-beta.jar`.

Verification tasks:

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat verifyAssets      # models, textures, recipes, effect icons
.\gradlew.bat verifyTypes       # drink-type classifier
.\gradlew.bat verifyChains      # full headless production chains
.\gradlew.bat verifySimulation  # gradual rectification and mass-balance smoke test
```

## Drink types

The type is never picked in a menu - it is derived from the production technology and raw material:

| Type | How it is made | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Wort** | Unfermented sweet wort; an ingredient, not a drink | Sweet and raw; not a drink yet, just the base for fermentation |
| **Fermented wash** | Fermented wort; drinkable, but rough and low quality | Cloudy and rough; drinkable, but better distilled |
| **Moonshine** | Whole run without fraction separation, or unaged grain/sugar hearts | A plain home distillate with no fraction cut: simple and strong |
| **Chacha** | Berry hearts after a fractional pot run, no oak | A clear berry distillate with cuts but no oak |
| **Cachaca** | Sugar-cane hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A grassy, sharp cane distillate |
| **Potato spirit** | Potato hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A neutral distillate with an earthy finish |
| **Beet spirit** | Beetroot hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A faintly sweet, unusual distillate |
| **Mead** | Honey hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A floral, soft honey distillate |
| **Cocoa spirit** | Cocoa-bean hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A dark, chocolate-scented distillate |
| **Melon spirit** | Melon hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A light, green-fruited distillate |
| **Carrot spirit** | Carrot hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | An earthy-sweet garden distillate |
| **Cactus spirit** | Cactus hearts after a fractional pot run, with or without oak | A bright, vegetal desert distillate |
| **Brandy** | Berry hearts after oak aging, one distillation | A single-pass berry distillate matured on oak |
| **Cognac** | Berry hearts after oak aging, two or more distillations | A double-pass berry distillate matured on oak |
| **Calvados** | Oak-aged apple hearts | An apple distillate with a soft orchard aroma |
| **Whiskey** | Oak-aged grain hearts, one distillation | A grain distillate matured on oak chips |
| **Scotch** | Oak-aged grain hearts, two or more distillations | A smoky, full-bodied double-pass grain distillate |
| **Rum** | Oak-aged sugar hearts | A sugar distillate matured on oak; warm and caramel-sweet |
| **Vodka** | Rectified, filtered and diluted spirit | Rectified, filtered spirit diluted to drinking strength |
| **Spirit** | Rectified spirit before filtration or dilution | High-strength rectified spirit: dilute or reprocess it |

Blending additives only change the name and aroma; the base type is preserved.

A few further types (gin, absinthe, liqueur, tincture) already carry names and descriptions but are placeholders: the current production chain cannot reach them yet. Calvados, cachaca, potato spirit, beet spirit and mead are now reachable from the added raw materials.

## Tasting table

The tasting table reads a finished drink without touching it. Place a bottle in the drink slot and a hydrometer in the tool slot; the panel then reports the derived **type**, volume, strength, quality and impurities, and what a glass would do - intoxication level I-V, duration, the infusion aura and technical-fraction debuffs. The sample is never consumed, so the same bottle can be checked before and after aging. An empty hand with nothing loaded prints the same summary to chat.

Craft it from three oak planks across the top, a glass bottle in the middle and copper ingot / paper / copper ingot along the bottom.

## Safety

This is a game, not an instruction. Ethanol is flammable and toxic fractions are genuinely dangerous; real distillation requires ventilation, pressure control, fraction separation and compliance with local law. The mechanics here are deliberately simplified and **must not be reproduced in real life**.
